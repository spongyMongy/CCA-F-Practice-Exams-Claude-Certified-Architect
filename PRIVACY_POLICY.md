# Privacy Policy — Architect Elite: Claude Exam

**Effective Date:** July 25, 2026

## 1. Overview
"Architect Elite: Claude Exam" (the "App"), developed by Arslan, is an offline-first educational tool designed to help users prepare for professional certifications. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## 2. No Data Collection
**We do not collect any personal information.**
*   **No Accounts:** You do not need to create an account to use the App.
*   **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (like Firebase or Mixpanel) that track your behavior or "phone home."
*   **No Personal Data:** We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or device location.

## 3. Local Data Storage
All study progress, quiz results, and settings are stored **locally on your device** using an internal database. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by the developer or any third parties.

## 4. Third-Party Services (Google Play Billing)
The App uses the **Google Play Billing Library** to process one-time purchases for "Elite" content.
*   When you make a purchase, Google manages the transaction.
*   We do not receive or store your credit card information or billing address.
*   Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details on how they handle payment information.

## 5. Security
Since all data is stored locally, the security of your study data depends on the security of your mobile device. We recommend using standard device security features (like PIN or Biometrics).

## 6. Children's Privacy
The App does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in a new version of the App and an updated effective date.

## 8. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the support link on the Google Play Store.
